## Safety and a Sensible Buying Checklist

Before adding an at-home LED skincare device to your routine:

- Read the manual for the exact model and mode; do not transfer an eight-minute schedule between listings by assumption.
- Follow the supplied eye-protection directions. Ask a qualified professional about eye conditions, photosensitivity or photosensitizing medicines before use.
- Use products alongside the mask only if the instructions permit it; a serum is not automatically compatible with every light device.
- Stop if you develop pain, eye symptoms, persistent irritation or unusual skin changes, and seek appropriate advice.
- Compare the final price with warranty scope, regional service, return eligibility and any hygiene-related return exclusions.

None of these masks should replace prescribed care. If your main concern is a skin condition, seek qualified guidance rather than relying on a mode called “Clear.”
