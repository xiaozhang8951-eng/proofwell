## FDA Clearance and Safety

The owner supplied **K260511** as the FDA source to inspect, with proposed indications of full-face wrinkles and mild-to-moderate inflammatory acne. **Those indications, the device identity and the clearance decision have not been independently confirmed here.** The document must be read and matched to UM10 before a definitive clearance or indication statement is published.<Citation number={1} />

Under the 510(k) framework, a clearance concerns substantial equivalence for the stated intended use; **FDA clearance is not FDA approval**. Even a confirmed clearance must not be expanded into proof of all advertised benefits, superiority, universal suitability or an independently tested result in this review. The FDA's institutional explanation is the source to check for that regulatory distinction.<Citation number={4} />

Read the current, region-appropriate UM10 manual for positioning, eye protection, exposure limits, charging, cleaning and contraindications.
