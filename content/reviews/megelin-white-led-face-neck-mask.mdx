## Key Ingredients or Technology

This is a light-device assessment rather than an active-ingredient review. No companion skincare formulation or active ingredient has been verified.

LEDs deliver light within selected wavelength bands. For red and near-infrared applications, photobiomodulation research examines interactions with endogenous photoacceptors and downstream signaling. Cytochrome c oxidase is **one proposed target**, not the only established explanation for every effect. Cellular changes in an experimental system cannot independently establish visible benefits from this mask.<Citation number={2} />

**Red light and near-infrared light are separate spectral regions.** Red is visible; near-infrared is not. Their absorption and scattering in tissue differ, but greater transmission does not automatically establish a better cosmetic outcome. The wording “red light” in a product name does not confirm that the same product also emits near-infrared light.

Our [How LED Face Masks Work](/science/how-led-masks-work) and [Red vs Near-Infrared Light](/science/red-vs-near-infrared) explain these concepts. ## Dose or Technical Parameters

Wavelength describes only part of an exposure. Irradiance concerns incident optical power per area at a stated position; exposure duration and output over time determine accumulated surface radiant exposure. For steady output, radiant exposure equals irradiance in W/cm² multiplied by seconds. It is not a direct measurement of energy absorbed by a particular tissue target.

The measurement method, source-to-skin distance, mask curvature, distribution across the face and neck, and operating mode all matter. The radiometry literature is useful background for these questions, rather than a source of specifications for an unmeasured mask.<Citation number={4} />

Read [Irradiance vs Fluence](/science/irradiance-vs-fluence) before comparing headline numbers. Neither a higher advertised LED count nor a larger exposure number independently establishes superior performance. Without verified output and instructions, this review cannot provide a treatment duration or dose recommendation.
